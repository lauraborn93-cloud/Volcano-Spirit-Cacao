Cookie Policy

What we store in your browser, why, and how to change your mind at any time.

1. What are cookies

Cookies (and similar technologies like local storage) are small pieces of data a website stores on your device to remember information between visits or pages. We use them sparingly, and never set anything beyond what's strictly necessary until you give us permission.

2. Categories we use

CategoryPurposeCan you opt out?
Necessary Remembering the contents of your shopping bag and your cookie preference itself. The Site can't function without these. No, these are essential and never require consent under ePrivacy law
Analytics Understanding, in aggregate, which pages are useful and how visitors move through the Site. Yes, off by default until you opt in
Marketing Measuring and improving how we reach people on other platforms (e.g. social media). Yes, off by default until you opt in

3. Cookies currently set

As of the date at the top of this page, this Site sets only necessary, first-party storage: your shopping bag contents and your cookie preference choice, both stored locally in your browser (not shared with any third party). We are not currently running analytics or advertising scripts. If that changes, this page, and the categories in our consent banner, will be updated first, and any new analytics or marketing cookies will remain off until you actively opt in.

4. Managing your preferences

You can change your cookie choices at any time by clicking "Cookie Preferences" in the footer of any page. You can also clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings, which will reset your preference and show the consent banner again on your next visit.

5. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies? Email hello@atitlanspiritcacao.com. See also our Privacy Policy.

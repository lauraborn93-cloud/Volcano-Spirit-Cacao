Why Partner With Us

The same cacao, sourced directly from the families who grow it.

Every wholesale order traces back to the same small-scale Mayan families we work with directly in Guatemala, hand-harvested, sun-dried, fermented, and stone-ground with nothing added and nothing removed.

  • Special wholesale pricing and flexible order sizes
  • Direct trade from our farmers to your community, and an origin story that your people for sure want to hear
  • The highest quality ceremonial-grade cacao, without pesticide use, from volcanic soil so rich in flavor and nutrients
  • Available in 454 g and 1 kg blocks, custom sizes on request
